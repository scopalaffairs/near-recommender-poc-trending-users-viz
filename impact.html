Did the recommender change who gained followers?

    New followers per week (20 trending hubs vs 20 comparison accounts)

    Method: difference in differences. Hubs are the 20 accounts of the original trending list; the comparison group is the 20 largest accounts outside that list on 2023-04-15. Each count is the number of follows written to the NEAR blockchain (contract social.near) in the week; the recommender launch (approx. 2023-11-07) is dated from block heights and is approximate. The ratio compares the average after the launch to the average before it, hubs divided by comparison; the range (wide, so a small effect cannot be excluded) is a bootstrap over accounts, and the placebo values apply the same calculation to fake launch dates. No impression logs exist, so this measures follower growth, not who saw a recommendation. Weekly follows timeline · Graph
